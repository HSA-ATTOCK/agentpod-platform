# User guide

## Getting started

1. Sign up and confirm your email. Your account is reviewed by our team; you'll get an email when it's approved.
2. Open **Ask Baba** and describe what you need in plain words. Attach files if useful.
3. Baba plans bigger jobs first and asks you to confirm. It shows its progress, and asks your OK before anything that sends, publishes or changes something.

You can switch chats or pages while Baba works; tasks keep running.

**Getting-started checklist.** A new chat shows a short checklist: tell Baba about your business, connect email and calendar, add a document, give a first task, turn on the daily briefing and invite a teammate. Steps tick off by themselves. Hide it with the ✕; it disappears when everything is done.

**Task ideas.** Under the message box are one-click ideas. They match what your workspace has connected (for example "Catch up on email" once Gmail or Outlook is connected). Clicking one sends it straight to Baba, which asks for any details it needs.

**Daily briefing.** Settings → Notifications → Daily briefing. Off by default. When on, you get a short summary at the hour you choose, in your time zone from your Profile: today's calendar, unread emails from the last 24 hours, overdue invoices (QuickBooks or Xero), shop orders (Shopify), and what Baba did (finished and failed tasks, approvals waiting, workflow runs, website form replies). It arrives in the bell menu and by email. "Send me one now" sends one straight away. Viewers can't turn it on, because it shows the workspace's email and money details. The briefing reads your connected tools directly and uses no AI, so it costs nothing.

## What Baba can do

- **Research** markets, competitors and any topic, with a source for every fact.
- **Write** reports, proposals, emails and presentations (Word, PDF, PowerPoint, Excel).
- **Analyse data** from spreadsheets or a connected database, with charts.
- **Finance** reports from your figures, keeping actual numbers separate from estimates.
- **Marketing & SEO**: website audits, plans, content and ad copy.
- **Websites**: design, build and test a site, with a preview link; publish it live when you approve.
- **Mobile apps**: an Expo app with a web preview and the full source code.
- **Your tools**: Gmail, Drive, Calendar, Slack, HubSpot, Outlook, QuickBooks, Xero, Shopify (the last three read-only), any service with an API key, and read-only PostgreSQL/MySQL.
- **Voice**: click the microphone in the chat box to dictate.

## Pages

| Page | For |
|---|---|
| Ask Baba | Chats. Use the search bar at the top to find any chat, message, task, file or workflow. Edit your last message with the pencil. |
| Dashboard / Activity | What Baba did, cost and results. |
| AI Agents | Baba's specialists, and **your own agents**: create a specialist (or start from a template) with your instructions, model (Claude, or OpenAI/Gemini if your platform allows them), web access, connected tools (read-only) and cost limit. |
| Workflows | Saved tasks: run on demand, on a schedule (your workspace's time zone) or from a webhook link. Add steps: *Do*, *Only continue if*, *Ask me first*, *Notify me*; drag them into order and see the flow diagram. Every change is kept as a version. |
| Websites | Preview links, version history, restore, publish live, your own domain (follow the DNS steps shown, then Check domain), form messages from your site with CSV download. |
| Memory | Facts Baba remembers about your business, and a knowledge base (PDF, Word, text files or pasted text) it searches. |
| Integrations | Connect Google, Slack, HubSpot, Microsoft 365, QuickBooks, Xero, Shopify, API keys and databases. |
| Usage & billing | Spend against your limits, plan allowances, plans, invoices. |
| Settings / Profile | Workspace, team and roles, notifications, two-step verification, download your data, delete your account. |

## Roles

Owner and Admin manage settings, team, connections, billing and websites. Members give Baba tasks. Viewers can look but not run tasks.

## Your data

Secrets you add (keys, passwords) are encrypted and never shown to the AI. Download everything from Profile → Download my data.
